99.9% Caffeine-Free

An example of how something can be true and still bullshit after a fashion.

Nestle’s Hot Cocoa mix: With rich chocolate flavor and only 20 calories per single-serve packet, this cocoa makes a 99.9% caffeine-free, 8 fl oz serving. A 20oz Starbucks drip coffee has ~21mg of caffeine per ounce, and thus 0.075% caffeine by weight – strong coffee is also 99.9% caffeine-free!
