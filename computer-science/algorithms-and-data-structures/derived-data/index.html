Derived Data Systems

Dated Aug 1, 2026; last modified on Mon, 10 Aug 2026

Systems of record hold the authoritative version of your data. Derived data systems are the result of taking some existing data from another system and transforming it in some way. For example, in recommendation systems, predictive summary data is often derived from usage logs. Being clear about which data is derived from which other data helps bring clarity to a system’s architecture.

Designing Data-Intensive Applications: The big ideas behind reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems. Part III: Derived Data. Kleppmann, Martin. Mar 16, 2017. ISBN: 978-1098119065 .
